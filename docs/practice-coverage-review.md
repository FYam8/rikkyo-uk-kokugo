The historical 24-unit artifact was not recovered. This is a **new app-authored** bank, not a restoration: 24 units, 175 items (10 kanji, 7 literary, 7 expository units). All passages, answers, extraction lengths and written rubrics were reviewed. The original 168 IDs remain; K09 adds seven language items. No FY26-B question or explanation is a practice source.

## Learning design

The learner starts with a full visible past paper. Wrong answers determine seven repair strands; the learner must retry an original question, then complete basic, transfer and mixed checks. A separate reserved item becomes available after 24 hours. Failure starts a new delay. The next past paper checks performance on long, unfamiliar source material. Short practice passages isolate a skill; they do not replace full-paper reading.

Every strand has at least two basic, three transfer, one mixed and one reserved retention item. Transfer, mixed and retention use material distinct from the basic passage. The runtime tests check actual item formats and grading, not merely declared unit tags. Desktop and mobile browser training exercises all seven strands, written self-assessment, original-question replay, all STEP stages and the elapsed-time gate.

## Fine-grained source demands

The 26 source taxonomy tags without a literal item tag are not represented as 26 completed specialist courses. Their treatment is:

| Demands | Treatment and limit |
|---|---|
| ATTITUDE_CHANGE, RELATIONSHIP_MAP | L01–03 track actions, speakers and changes; source replay retains the original relationship-map format. |
| CAUSAL_EXPLANATION, COMPARISON, CONCEPT_COMPARISON, CONCEPT_EXPLANATION, CONCEPT_RELATION, EXAMPLE_IDENTIFICATION, PARAPHRASE, THEME, THEME_SUMMARY | L06–07/E01/E04–05 require reasons, comparisons, main ideas and paraphrases with required elements. These are broader reading strands, not one independent module per tag. |
| PARAGRAPH_ROLE, PARAGRAPH_STRUCTURE, TRANSFER_APPLICATION | E01 identifies the role of examples, E03 logical links, E05 compares two materials, E07 sequence/insertion. Long paragraph organization is checked in the subsequent full past paper. |
| GRAMMAR_PATTERN, HOMOPHONE | K09 covers subject/predicate, modifiers, word class and meaning; K01–02 and K10 distinguish contextual homophones. Not an exhaustive grammar or homophone syllabus. |
| DIAGRAM_COMPLETION, DIALOGUE_COMPLETION, NOTE_COMPLETION, TABLE_COMPLETION, REFERENCE_READING | Exact source formats remain in past papers and original-question replay. E06 practices reading a displayed table and proportions. New bank does not claim a separate interactive diagram/table-fill course. |
| AGRICULTURE_TERM, BODY_PART, CULTURAL_KNOWLEDGE, HISTORICAL_CAUSATION, BIAS_CONCEPT | Text-specific knowledge is explained in verified source review. Contextual vocabulary and logical inference are practiced elsewhere; these narrow content areas are not claimed as a comprehensive knowledge bank. |

Explanations identify correct content and refer back to the passage; several are concise rather than exhaustive per-distractor essays. Written items require explicit element-by-element self-assessment and allow equivalent wording. Automated keyword matching is not used to certify written mastery.

## Evidence limits

No measured admissions outcome or guaranteed score increase is claimed. Initial transfer passages are shorter than entrance-exam texts. Some small strands have one reserved next-day item, which may recur after later repairs; full-paper cross-checks provide additional unseen evaluation. FY26-B remains outside practice/Today and its answer manifest is loaded only after final submission. Static hosting provides application-level isolation, not access control against a user inspecting asset URLs.
